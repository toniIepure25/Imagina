## Manuscript authorship

Authorship is decided by substantive contribution, not by title or funding. Before submission:

- [ ] PI confirmed in writing.
- [ ] Author list and order agreed by all authors.
- [ ] Affiliations and ORCIDs complete.
- [ ] CRediT roles assigned.
- [ ] COI disclosures collected.
- [ ] Funding acknowledged.
- [ ] Data/code contribution statement.
- [ ] Corresponding author assigned.
