# Financial model

**Status:** illustrative planning model. No revenue, pricing validation, or customer commitment exists.
All figures are hypotheses to be replaced by interview and pilot data.

## 1. Revenue hypotheses

| Offer | Unit | Illustrative price | Evidence status |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Self-hosted lab licence | lab/year | €6,000 | untested |
| Pilot / study setup | engagement | €15,000 | untested |
| Analysis/validation support | project | €10,000–€25,000 | untested |
| Enterprise deployment | team/year | €25,000+ | untested |

Do not present these as market prices. They are anchors for customer-discovery questions.

## 2. Three-year scenarios

| Scenario | Paying labs by year 3 | Licence revenue | Services revenue | Total revenue |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Conservative | 10 | €60,000 | €30,000 | €90,000 |
| Base | 30 | €180,000 | €90,000 | €270,000 |
| Ambitious | 100 | €600,000 | €300,000 | €900,000 |

Assumptions: annual churn 10–20%; services are founder-led; no clinical reimbursement; no consumer
subscription. These are not forecasts.

## 3. Cost hypotheses (18 months)

| Cost | Range | Note |
|---|---:|---|
| Founder time | opportunity cost | not a cash cost if bootstrapped |
| Cloud/GPU benchmark | €3,000–€8,000 | target-scale SBC/recovery |
| Ethics/legal/privacy | €5,000–€15,000 | entity, DPA, review |
| Design/demo/website | €3,000–€8,000 | contractor |
| Travel/conferences | €3,000–€6,000 | design partners |
| Contingency | €5,000–€10,000 | |
| **Cash total** | **€19,000–€47,000** | excluding founder salary |

A funded 18-month plan with one salary is a different model and requires grant or seed support.

## 4. Milestones tied to money

1. Five interviews → refine offer.
2. Three paid pilots/LOIs → validate willingness to pay.
3. Target-scale CRT PASS/FAIL → de-risk the science.
4. Ethics/registration → unlock human study.
5. Methods paper submission → credibility.
6. Seed/angel conversation only after 2–3.

## 5. Grant vs equity

Grants are non-dilutive and fit the research stage. Equity before LOIs would price the company on a
deck, not evidence. The recommended sequence is grants and paid pilots first.

## 6. What would falsify the model

- Fewer than three labs willing to pay after 20 interviews.
- Procurement cycles longer than nine months for a €6k licence.
- Labs require cloud/multi-tenant features that contradict the local-first design.
- The CRT design remains non-identifiable after target-scale work.

If these occur, pivot to grant-funded open tooling or study services; do not force a SaaS story.
