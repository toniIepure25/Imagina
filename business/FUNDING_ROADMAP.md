# Funding roadmap

**Status:** planning only. Eligibility and deadlines must be checked against the current call text.
This is not legal or financial advice.

## 1. Sequence

1. **Non-dilutive research funding** for target-scale compute, calibration, and ethics.
2. **Paid pilots/LOIs** for product evidence.
3. **Methods paper** for credibility.
4. **Seed/angel** only after pilots and a validated instrument.

Equity before evidence would price the company on a narrative, not on data.

## 2. Instruments

### EIC Pathfinder

- Stage: TRL 1–4.
- Funding: up to €4M; consortia generally required for Pathfinder Open.
- Fit: target-scale inference, calibration, human study, academic partnership.
- Current information: [EIC Pathfinder](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-pathfinder_en).
- Action: identify a university/ethics partner and a compute partner before writing.

### EIC Accelerator

- Stage: TRL 6–8.
- Funding: grant up to €2.5M plus equity/blended finance.
- Fit: later, after a validated product and paid pilots.
- Current information: [EIC Accelerator](https://eic.ec.europa.eu/eic-funding-opportunities/eic-accelerator_en).
- Action: do not apply now; the product is not at TRL 6–8.

### NSF SBIR/STTR

- Stage: early-stage deep tech.
- Funding: up to $2M seed, zero equity.
- Eligibility: US small business; verify current rules.
- Current information: [NSF SBIR](https://seedfund.nsf.gov/).
- Action: only if a US entity is established.

### University and national instruments

- Ethics/recruitment partnership.
- National research grants for the human study.
- Compute allocations via a university or national HPC center.
- Verify eligibility and deadlines locally; do not assume a call exists.

## 3. Application package

- One-pager and deck.
- Scientific protocol and Stage 0J artifact.
- Ethics/DPIA and data management plan.
- Budget and timeline.
- Team, advisors, and COI.
- Letters of support from design partners (when real).
- Risk register and contingency.

## 4. Grant narrative (draft)

> We build local-first instrumentation that makes imagination research auditable. The scientific
> bottleneck is not image generation; it is the inability to separate signal strength, precision, and
> active prediction in source monitoring. We will calibrate a target-scale Bayesian design, run a
> preregistered adversarial study, and release an open benchmark. The commercial path is a self-hosted
> lab instrument plus study support, sold first to research labs and neurotech teams.

## 5. Red flags to avoid

- Claiming clinical or decoding benefit.
- Presenting a simulation as human evidence.
- Counting a grant as revenue.
- Naming advisors or partners without consent.
- Submitting a consortium without a written role and budget.
