# Pitch deck outline

**Status:** draft. This is a speaking outline, not a claim that the company is funded or validated.
Every number must be traceable to a repository artifact or an interview note.

## Slide 1 — Title

IMAGINA/ANIMUS — make imagination inspectable.
Local-first research instrumentation. Pre-traction. Seeking design partners and non-dilutive funding.

## Slide 2 — Problem

Imagery research is assembled from disconnected tools. The scene, task, exclusion rule, and model
assumption are difficult to audit. Generative tools optimize output, not the process.

## Slide 3 — Product

Studio, Lumen, and Evidence on one local backend. Immutable versions, explicit uncertainty, exportable
provenance.

## Slide 4 — Demo

Three-minute screen recording: describe, stage, correct, compare, export. Show the claim boundary in
the product, not only in the deck.

## Slide 5 — Why now

Growth in imagery/aphantasia research, privacy pressure in neurotech, and reproducibility requirements.
No market-size assertion without a bottom-up source.

## Slide 6 — Evidence

- Engineering: CI and ledger.
- Science: simulation-only; target-scale NUTS/SBC blocked; zero human participants.
- Commercial: zero interviews/LOIs recorded.

This slide is deliberately uncomfortable. It is the reason the next milestone is a pilot, not a seed
round.

## Slide 7 — Beachhead

Research labs and neurotech teams. Buyer: PI, lab manager, or research lead. Budget owner: grant or
study budget.

## Slide 8 — Business model

Self-hosted lab licence, study setup, analysis services. Pricing hypotheses only; validate with five
interviews and three pilots.

## Slide 9 — Competition

PsychoPy/Gorilla/jsPsych for task delivery; Unity/Blender for scenes; generative image tools for output.
Differentiation: one loop, local provenance, explicit evidence status. Do not claim superiority without
a comparative study.

## Slide 10 — Milestones

1. Five design partners.
2. Three paid pilots/LOIs.
3. Target-scale CRT readiness.
4. Ethics/registration.
5. Methods paper submission.
6. Seed/angel only after pilots.

## Slide 11 — Team and advisors

Founder, PI, independent statistician, ethics partner, adversarial collaborators. Fill names before
sending. No fictional advisors.

## Slide 12 — Ask

Three pilot partners; one ethics partner; grant/compute support. Use of funds: calibration, target-scale
compute, security/release, customer discovery.
