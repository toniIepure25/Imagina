# Customer discovery

**Status:** protocol only. No interviews have been recorded in this repository. Do not mark a segment
as validated until the notes exist.

## 1. Segments

| Segment | Buyer | Job to be done | Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Imagery/aphantasia labs | PI | Run a defensible imagery task without building tooling | primary |
| Psychology methods labs | PI/research lead | Teach and test source-monitoring models | primary |
| Neurotech/BCI teams | research lead | Behavioural ground truth and provenance | secondary |
| Pro creators | individual | Compose and revise scenes | adjacency; not the first market |

## 2. Interview script (45 minutes)

1. Walk me through the last imagery/source-monitoring study you ran.
2. Which tools did you use, and where did the data live?
3. What broke or required manual repair?
4. How do you decide that a version of a stimulus is the one you analysed?
5. What would a reviewer ask that you cannot currently show?
6. Who approves a new tool purchase, and from which budget?
7. What did you spend on the last study's software/setup?
8. What would make you reject a local-first tool?
9. Would you run a paid pilot? At what scope?
10. Who else should I speak with?

Do not pitch in the first 30 minutes. Record verbatim notes with consent.

## 3. Scorecard

| Criterion | 1 | 3 | 5 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pain frequency | annual | quarterly | weekly |
| Budget | none | grant line | existing software budget |
| Procurement | >9 months | 3–9 months | <3 months |
| Privacy fit | cloud required | mixed | local required |
| Willingness to pilot | no | maybe | signed LOI |

## 4. Decision gate

After 20 interviews:

- **Proceed** if at least five labs score ≥3 on pain and budget, and at least three accept a paid pilot
  or LOI.
- **Narrow** if one segment dominates; rewrite the one-pager for that segment.
- **Stop** if labs will not pay and will not pilot; keep the project as grant-funded open tooling.
