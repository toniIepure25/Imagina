# Business workspace

**Status:** pre-traction. No customer, LOI, paid pilot, grant, or investor commitment exists. These
documents are hypotheses and operating plans, not evidence of demand or revenue.

## Position

IMAGINA/ANIMUS is **local-first research instrumentation for imagination and source monitoring**: a
symbolic scene studio, behavioural measurement surfaces, and a provenance ledger that a lab can audit.
It is not a consumer image generator, a clinical product, a BCI, or a decoder of imagined content.

## Recommended beachhead

Research labs and neurotech teams that need an inspectable behavioural assay and a defensible data
trail. The first paid offer is a self-hosted lab deployment plus study setup, not a subscription SaaS.
