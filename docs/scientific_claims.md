# Scientific Claims Policy

## Allowed Claims

- "IMAGINA estimates proxy metrics related to attention, self-reported vividness, behavioral stability, and simulated EEG-like signal patterns."
- "IMAGINA adapts visual feedback based on estimated imagery stability."
- "IMAGINA is a research prototype for closed-loop mental imagery training."
- "IMAGINA does not decode thoughts or dreams."
- "The generated/procedural visuals are feedback scaffolds, not literal reconstructions of mental images."
- "PID and IQI are experimental proxy metrics that require validation."

## Prohibited Claims

The following must NEVER appear in UI, documentation, or marketing:

- "Reads your mind."
- "Decodes your dreams."
- "Shows what you are imagining."
- "Cures aphantasia."
- "Guarantees lucid dreaming."
- "Diagnoses mental health conditions."
- "Improves the brain clinically."
- "Controls reality."
- "Measures consciousness objectively."

## Metric Limitations

- IQI and PID are composite proxy metrics derived from self-report and simulated signals.
- V1 uses no real neural data — all EEG-like features are simulated.
- Metrics have not been validated in controlled experiments.
- Self-report components are inherently subjective.
- Results should not be used for clinical decision-making.

## Future Validation Plan

1. Compare IQI with validated imagery questionnaires (VVIQ, OSIVQ).
2. Correlate PID with EEG alpha power during imagery tasks.
3. Test curriculum progression against fixed-difficulty controls.
4. Conduct formal within-subjects repeated-measures experiments.
5. Publish findings with full methods, code, and data transparency.
