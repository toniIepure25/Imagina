# CRT ethics package

**Status:** template. No ethics approval exists. Do not recruit participants until an approved
protocol, consent form, and data management plan are in place.

## 1. Study summary

A behavioural study of source monitoring and mental imagery. Participants complete a computer task,
make source judgments, and report confidence. No neural data are collected. The study is not clinical
and does not provide a diagnosis or treatment.

## 2. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Fatigue | 240 trials split into blocks; breaks; stop rule |
| Frustration | practice block; clear instructions; withdrawal without penalty |
| Demand characteristics | yoked controls; non-diagnostic prediction arm; debrief |
| Privacy | local-first data; pseudonymous IDs; no raw neural data |
| Coercion | compensation not contingent on completion; students not recruited by the PI directly |

## 3. Consent

Consent must state:

- purpose and procedure;
- voluntary participation and withdrawal;
- compensation;
- data types and retention;
- who can access data;
- risks and benefits;
- contact for questions and complaints;
- that the study is not clinical and does not decode imagined content.

## 4. Data protection

- GDPR role: controller/processor to be named.
- Legal basis: consent and research exemption, confirmed by the institution.
- Data minimisation: no names, no raw EEG, no unnecessary demographics.
- Storage: encrypted local disk; access limited to named researchers.
- Retention: pre-specified; deletion procedure.
- Transfer: no external inference service; any transfer requires a DPA.
- Breach: institutional procedure and 72-hour assessment.

## 5. Inclusion and compensation

- Adults 18+.
- Normal or corrected-to-normal vision.
- No exclusion based on aphantasia status unless pre-registered.
- Compensation at the institutional rate; not contingent on completing all trials.

## 6. Incidental findings

No neural or clinical data are collected, so incidental clinical findings are not expected. If a
participant reports distress, the session stops and the participant is directed to appropriate support.

- [ ] Data management plan.

## 8. What must not be written

Do not state that the study is approved, registered, or run until the documents exist. Do not promise
clinical benefit or a decoded image.
