# CRT Literature Positioning

**Status:** rapid, non-systematic evidence review completed 2026-10-30. It supports design choices,
not a priority claim. Exact-phrase search counts are gap indicators, not proof of novelty. The
Stage 0I metadata and correction record is [CRT_SOURCE_AUDIT.md](CRT_SOURCE_AUDIT.md).

## 1. Positioning statement

CRT is best framed as a test of **prospective, test-selection-based reality monitoring**:

> Does predicting the consequence of a source-diagnostic test, and observing whether the prediction
> was wrong, change how people attribute an experience to seeing, imagining, describing, or novelty —
> and does dual counterfactual prediction help discrimination while also increasing false-seen
> attribution?

The contribution is not a new brain region or a validated clinical instrument. It is a falsifiable
behavioural-computational paradigm that links active evidence selection, prediction error, and source
attribution.

The direct-combination count is a gap indicator, not priority evidence. The 2025–2026 literature also
contains the **signal-strength** account (Dijkstra & Fleming 2023; Dijkstra et al. 2025), the
**higher-order precision** account (Dijkstra & Fleming preprint; Fleming 2026), and broader active
inference frameworks (Parr et al. 2022). Bayesian adversarial collaboration (Corcoran et al. 2023) is
the adjudication method, not evidence for a source account. CRT must beat these accounts, not only a
no-prediction baseline. The Stage 0H design-optimization gate is currently `REVIEW`, which is consistent with the
possibility that the current task cannot separate them; that is a finding about the design, not
evidence for CRT. The three-account decision table is in
[`CRT_THEORY_ADJUDICATION.md`](../program/CRT_THEORY_ADJUDICATION.md); the review protocol is in
[`CRT_STAGE0G_SCOPING_PROTOCOL.md`](CRT_STAGE0G_SCOPING_PROTOCOL.md).

## 4. Design implications

1. **Primary prediction contrast must be yoked.** Active prediction changes test selection, so
   `active_predict` versus `active_intervene` is a package effect, not a clean prediction effect.
2. **Do not rely on source confidence alone.** Model criterion bias and metacognitive sensitivity.
3. **Measure both benefit and cost.** Source accuracy and false-seen rate for imagined items are
   co-primary across the program; a benefit on one does not cancel harm on the other.
4. **Keep meta-EIG descriptive.** The Stage 0E tournament did not establish normative superiority.
5. **Stage 1D is the direct human test.** Single vs dual vs dual-nondiagnostic prediction on a fixed
   sequence is the direct human assay of the counterfactual mechanism.
6. **No priority claim.** The phrase “counterfactual reality testing” is a provisional label, not a
   discovered natural kind.
