# Code of conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1**
(https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code
of conduct.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback on code and on scientific claims.
- Critique ideas and evidence, not people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct errors openly.
- Respect privacy: do not request or share participant data, and do not
  deanonymize anyone.
- Do not use the project to make clinical, diagnostic, or mind-reading claims.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Deliberately misrepresenting results, suppressing negative findings, or
  pressuring contributors to overstate a claim.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards. They
may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, and issues that violate
this policy, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors.

Report incidents privately to the maintainers listed in `GOVERNANCE.md`. Reports
will be reviewed promptly and fairly; the privacy and safety of the reporter
will be respected.
