## 2. Background & aim
An independent, preregistered test of whether imagery of naturalistic videos evokes reliable,
imagery-specific patterns in the Wang2015 topographic visual ROI, above cue/post-video contamination
(design frozen in `results/c3xra/` and `reports/c3xra/C3XRP_PREREGISTRATION_DRAFT.md`). The prior internal
C3XAT-R1 assessment (LIMITED) **motivates** the replication but does **not** determine recruitment,
inclusion, thresholds, or analysis.

## 3. Design
- Healthy adults, `{{N}}` evaluable (recruit up to ceiling), 3 T MRI, multi-session imagery + perception
  runs (see `MRI_ACQUISITION_SPEC.md`, `results/c3xra/participant_plan.json`).

## 4. Recruitment & eligibility
- Community sample; **no** enrichment for vividness, imagery ability, or prior responsiveness; **not**
  targeting or resembling any specific prior individual. Standard MRI safety eligibility only
  (`MRI_SAFETY_SCREENING_DRAFT.md`).
- Inclusion: adult, MRI-safe, able to consent. Exclusion: MRI contraindication, inability to consent,
  `{{OTHER_MEDICAL}}`. **No** exclusion on neural/behavioral performance.

## 5. Consent
- Written informed consent (`CONSENT_FORM_DRAFT.md`) after the information sheet
  (`PARTICIPANT_INFORMATION_DRAFT.md`) and time to ask questions. Voluntary; withdrawal without penalty.
