## What is this study?
We are studying how the brain represents imagined and seen short videos, using MRI. This is an independent
replication of a specific brain-imaging analysis. `{{INSTITUTION}}`, PI `{{PI_NAME}}`.

## Do I have to take part?
No. Taking part is entirely voluntary. You can stop at any time, without giving a reason and without any
penalty or effect on your `{{care/studies/employment}}`.

## What will I do?
- A safety screening for MRI.
- `{{N_SESSIONS}}` MRI sessions of about `{{SESSION_MINUTES}}` minutes. Inside the scanner you will watch
  short video clips, imagine clips when cued, and occasionally press a button. There are no right or wrong
  answers, and your performance is not judged or used to decide whether your data are kept.

## Are there risks?
MRI is safe for people without contraindications (no incompatible metal/implants — the screening checks
this). The scanner is loud (you get ear protection) and enclosed. You can talk to us and stop at any time.
